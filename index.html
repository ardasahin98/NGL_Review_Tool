Consistency checks for Next Generation Liquefaction case histories

Pulls submitted-but-unreviewed sites through the NGL API, plots each test and flags what looks inconsistent – so reviewers can go straight to the problems.

  • CPT, boring logs & SPT, Vs profiles, field observations
  • Every finding marked Inconsistent, Not sure or Consistent, with depth / location
  • Reference PDFs and digitised figures shown side by side with the data
